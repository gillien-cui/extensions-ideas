# 🔍 Meta-Prompt: Independent Business Viability Analysis

*(Shopify CSV Import/Export & Data Validator)*

> **Role**
> You are a **skeptical but fair senior product strategist / VC analyst** evaluating whether a new Shopify app is worth building as a real business (not a hobby project).

> **Context**
> The proposed product is a **Shopify Bulk CSV Import/Export & Data Validator**:

* Validates Shopify CSV files before import
* Explains errors in human language
* Auto-fixes common issues (encoding, headers, variants, taxonomy)
* Provides dry-run previews and repeatable templates
* Targets merchants frustrated with Shopify CSV imports

You should assume:

* The founder is a solo technical founder
* Initial focus is SMB merchants
* Competing products include Ablestar Bulk Product Editor, Matrixify (Excelify), and Shopify’s native CSV import

---

## 🔎 Your Task

Conduct an **independent, critical evaluation** of this business idea.

### 1️⃣ Evidence gathering (required)

* Search **Reddit (r/shopify, r/ecommerce)**, Shopify Community, app reviews, blog posts etc.
* Identify **repeated, high-signal complaints** about:

  * CSV import failures
  * Shopify error messages
  * Excel/Google Sheets corruption
  * Variant / option / taxonomy issues
* Quote or summarize **at least 5 concrete examples** with links or citations

⚠️ Do not rely on assumptions. Show evidence.

---

### 2️⃣ Market & demand analysis

* Estimate:

  * How common CSV import workflows are among Shopify merchants
  * How frequently failures occur
  * Whether this pain is **acute** or **annoying**
* Compare:

  * Merchants who already use bulk editors vs those who still rely on CSVs
* Answer: **Is this a frequent, revenue-impacting problem or a niche edge case?**

---

### 3️⃣ Competitive landscape (brutally honest)

For each competitor (Ablestar, Matrixify, Shopify native):

* What job are they actually hired to do?
* Where do they *already* solve this problem well?
* Where do users complain they fall short?
* Is there a **clear, defensible wedge** for a focused validator?

Include:

* Feature overlap
* Pricing comparisons
* Review-based complaints

---

### 4️⃣ Monetization & willingness-to-pay

* Based on evidence, estimate:

  * Likely price range merchants would tolerate
  * Whether merchants would pay *just* for validation / safety
* Identify:

  * Who would pay (SMB, agencies, ops teams)
  * Who would **not** pay and why

---

### 5️⃣ Risk assessment

Analyze the biggest risks:

* Platform risk (Shopify improving native import)
* Red-ocean app store dynamics
* Support cost vs pricing
* “One-time pain” vs recurring pain
* Feature creep pressure

Rank risks by severity.

---

### 6️⃣ Verdict (forced choice)

You must choose **one**:

* ✅ Worth building as a standalone business
* ⚠️ Only viable as a narrow niche / side business
* ❌ Not worth pursuing

Justify your verdict with **evidence**, not opinion.

---

### 7️⃣ If viable: success conditions

If you conclude it *is* viable:

* What must be true for this to succeed?
* What must the MVP absolutely nail?
* What would kill this business quickly?

If *not* viable:

* Suggest adjacent pivots or reframings that *would* be viable

---

## 📌 Output format (strict)

* Output in .md format
* Use clear section headers
* Include bullet points
* Include links or references where possible
* Avoid hype language
* Avoid generic startup advice

---

## 🧠 Evaluation Criteria

You are being evaluated on:

* Depth of reasoning
* Use of concrete data
* Willingness to say “no”
* Ability to distinguish **real pain vs perceived pain**

---

### (Optional) Follow-up prompt

After your analysis, propose **3 alternative Shopify app ideas** with:

* Clear pain
* Strong monetization
* Lower competitive pressure
