# Decathlon Backcountry Essentials — Must-Have Gear List

A buyer's guide to building a complete backcountry/backpacking kit from Decathlon. Decathlon's house brands (**Forclaz** for trekking, **Quechua** for camping/hiking, **Simond** for mountaineering) consistently punch far above their price. Note: Decathlon has been folding the trekking-focused Forclaz range into the **Simond** line, so the same product may appear under either brand depending on region and season.

Naming convention: **MT100** = entry level, **MT500** = mid-range, **MT900** = top of the line (lightest/warmest). For backcountry use, the MT900 line is usually worth it for the "big three"; MT500 is fine for everything else.

Prices below are approximate US/UK retail as of mid-2026 and shift with sales.

---

## The Big Three

### 1. Shelter — Forclaz MT900 tent family

Three configurations, all well reviewed:

| Model | Weight | Style | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| MT900 Trekking Tarp Tent | ~920 g | Trekking-pole tarp tent | Ultralight, already carrying poles |
| MT900 Dome (1P) | ~1,300 g | Freestanding dome | All-round wild camping (~£160) |
| MT900 UL Tunnel (2P/3P) | ~1.1–1.5 kg | Tunnel | Two people, windy conditions |

- 2,000 mm hydrostatic head fly — solid 3-season rain protection.
- Reviewers call the dome "a budget, no-frills version of MSR/Big Agnes/Sea to Summit designs without the price tag." Trade-off: interiors run narrow and materials feel more basic than premium brands.
- Pitches in under 5 minutes on first try.

**Budget alternative:** Quechua MH100/MH500 domes — heavier, fine for short approaches.

### 2. Sleep system

**Sleeping bag — Forclaz/Simond Trek MT900 Down:**

| Rating | Use case |
|---|---|
| MT900 10°C | Summer only, very light and packable |
| MT900 / Simond MT900 5°C | 3-season workhorse — "brilliant bang for your buck" |
| MT900 0°C | Shoulder season / cold sleepers — widely called the best-value down bag on the market |

- 640 CUIN (EU standard) duck down ≈ 700+ US fill power.
- 20D shell — more durable than the 7–10D fabrics on elite UL bags.
- 2-year warranty; packability rivals bags costing 2–3× more.

**Sleeping pad (don't skip — the pad is half your warmth):**

| Model | R-value | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| MT900 Inflatable | 5.4 | ~620 g | 4-season, packs to Nalgene size, ~£130 — comfort compared to Therm-a-Rest NeoAir at nearly half the price |
| MT500 Air | 1.5 | ~510 g | Summer only (nights above ~8°C) |
| MT500 Folding Foam | 2.2 | ~430 g | Puncture-proof CCF backup or budget option |

**Recommended default:** MT900 pad — the R-5.4 covers all three seasons and cold ground.

### 3. Pack — Forclaz MT900 Symbium 50+10 L

- 50 L main body + removable 10 L lid; ~1.3 kg (2.9 lb) total.
- Rated to carry ~12 kg (26.5 lb) comfortably; seven pockets, full-height front access.
- "AIR" suspended-mesh back panel for ventilation.
- Holds up well after a month of continuous travel/camping per long-term reviews.
- Women's-specific version available. For UL loads, Simond also sells a stripped-down MT900 UL 50 L.

## Kitchen & Water

- **Stove — MT500 compact gas stove (~85 g):** piezo igniter, built-in wind guard, boils 1 L in ~3 min. Screws onto standard threaded canisters. (~$30)
- **Cookset — MT500 stainless 1P (or Trek 500 1.6 L for two):** pot with locking folding handle, bowl, lid/strainer, folding spork. The whole kit nests a 230 g canister + stove inside. Stainless, not titanium — a bit heavier but cheap and near-indestructible.
- **Water treatment:** Decathlon sells its own trekking water filter plus purification tablets (Aquatabs-style). A squeeze filter (Decathlon's or a Sawyer) + tablets as backup is the standard setup.
- **Bottles/reservoir:** Forclaz soft flasks or 2 L hydration bladder; carry ≥2 L capacity.

## Clothing (layering)

- **Down jacket — MT100 hooded (~$110–120):** 800-fill, cult-favorite value; warm to ~-5°C with a fleece under it. Runs small — size up for layering. MT500 hoodless version layers better under a shell hood.
- **Rain shell — Forclaz MT500 waterproof jacket:** taped seams, pit zips; the value pick over big-brand Gore-Tex for 3-season use.
- **Fleece midlayer — Quechua MH120 / Forclaz MT100 fleece:** cheap, effective.
- **Base layers:** Forclaz merino tees and leggings — merino at roughly half the usual price is one of Decathlon's best-kept secrets.
- **Hiking pants, liner gloves, beanie, spare socks** (Forclaz merino hiking socks).

## The rest of the Ten Essentials

| Category | Decathlon pick |
|---|---|
| Navigation | Map + compass (Geonaute/Forclaz compass); phone + GPX app as primary, paper as backup |
| Headlamp | Forclaz HL500/HL900 USB-rechargeable, ~100+ lumens, + spare battery bank |
| First aid | Pre-built trekking first-aid kit, add blister care (Compeed) |
| Sun | Sunglasses (cat. 3+), sunscreen, cap |
| Knife/repair | Small folding knife, duct tape wrapped on pole, pad patch kit (included with MT900 pad) |
| Fire | Lighter ×2 (piezo stoves fail; always carry a lighter) |
| Emergency shelter | Survival blanket (~$5 at checkout aisle) |
| Trekking poles | Forclaz MT500 folding poles — double as tent poles for the tarp tent |
| Dry storage | Forclaz waterproof compression bags / pack liner |
| Sit/camp | Optional: Forclaz trekking pillow (~60 g, genuinely good) |

| MT900 5°C down bag | ~800 g | $150 |

| MT900 Symbium 50+10 pack | 1,300 g | $160 |
| MT500 stove + cookset + canister | ~600 g | $60 |
| Filter + bottles | ~250 g | $45 |
| MT100 down + MT500 shell + fleece | ~900 g | $220 |
| Headlamp, first aid, knife, misc | ~500 g | $80 |
| **Total (base weight ~5.5 kg)** | | **~$1,025** |

A comparable name-brand kit (MSR + Therm-a-Rest + Osprey + Patagonia) runs $2,500+. Decathlon gets you a sub-6 kg base weight for well under half that, with a 2-year warranty across the line.

## Where Decathlon is *not* the pick

- **Winter/4-season mountaineering shelters** — the MT900 line is 3-season; look at Simond's mountaineering range or specialist brands.
- **Trail runners / broken-in boots** — fit trumps price; try on in person (Decathlon shoes are fine if they fit you).
- **Titanium ultralight cookware** — Decathlon only does stainless/aluminum; TOAKS is the cheap titanium option.
