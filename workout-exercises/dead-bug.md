# Dead Bug

**Targets:** deep core, anti-extension (keeps your lower back stable) · **Equipment:** mat · **Used in:** swap for crunch · **Reps:** 2 × 10 per side

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Supine Dead Bug](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/147/supine-dead-bug/)

A back-friendly core exercise that also carries over well to climbing (keeping your body tight on overhangs).

## Setup
- Lie on your back. Arms straight up toward the ceiling, hips and knees bent at 90° (shins parallel to the floor).
- Press your lower back gently into the mat and brace.

## How to do it
1. Slowly lower your **right arm** overhead and straighten your **left leg** toward the floor at the same time.
2. Stop just before your lower back starts to lift off the mat.
3. Return to the start, then switch sides (left arm + right leg).
4. Breathe out as you extend.

## Cues
- Lower back stays glued to the mat the whole time.
- Slow: about 3 seconds out, 3 seconds back.

## Common mistakes
- Back arching off the mat → smaller range.
- Moving fast.
- Holding your breath.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** move only the legs, or only the arms; or tap your heel to the floor with your knee bent.
- **Harder:** hold a light dumbbell in your hands, or a slow 5-second extension.
