# Face Pull

**Targets:** rear shoulders, upper back, rotator cuff · **Equipment:** cable machine + rope · **Used in:** Day A · **Reps:** 2 × 12–15

**Demo (photos + video):** [NASM — Face Pull](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/face-pull)

Great for climbers: it strengthens the muscles that pull your shoulders back and rotate them outward. Climbing tends to round the shoulders forward. Keep it **light and controlled**; this is not an ego lift.

## Setup
- Set the cable pulley at or slightly above face height and attach the rope.
- Grab the rope with palms facing each other (thumbs toward you), step back until your arms are straight and the stack lifts slightly.
- Staggered stance or feet shoulder-width apart, slight lean back.

## How to do it
1. Pull the rope toward your face, splitting the ends apart so your hands finish beside your ears.
2. Elbows stay high (at or above shoulder height) and you rotate your hands back, like a "double bicep" pose.
3. Hold 1 second, squeezing your upper back.
4. Return slowly until your arms are straight.

## Cues
- "Pull the rope apart", not just back.
- Elbows high, knuckles back.
- No leaning back or jerking.

## Common mistakes
- Too heavy → turns into a row with body swing.
- Elbows dropping → becomes a row and misses the rear shoulders.
- Shrugging up into your ears → keep your shoulders down.

## Checking 沉肩 (shoulders down): am I using my traps?

Some trap work is good: the **middle and lower traps** pull your shoulder blades back and down. What you want to avoid is the **upper traps** shrugging your shoulders up toward your ears.

- **Set your shoulders before every rep.** With your arms straight, make your neck long and drop your shoulders away from your ears. Hold that position through the whole rep.
- **Ear–shoulder gap check:** that gap should stay the same from start to finish. If it shrinks, you're shrugging.
- **Touch check:** do a single-arm face pull and rest your other hand on the top of the working shoulder, between your neck and shoulder. It should stay soft, not bunch up.
- **Where you feel it:** the back of your shoulders and between your shoulder blades = right. The side of your neck = upper traps.
- **Film it:** take a side-view phone video and watch whether your shoulders rise.
- **Go lighter.** Too much weight is the #1 cause of shrugging.
- **Lower the pulley** to about chin or chest height if you still shrug, and keep your elbows level with your shoulders, not above.
- **Cue:** "put your shoulder blades in your back pockets."
- **Primer:** before your sets, do 10 band pull-aparts, or 10 straight-arm "shoulder drops" on the cable (arms straight, only move your shoulders down and up). This teaches the down position.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** lighter weight, or a resistance band anchored at face height.
- **Harder:** 2-second hold at the end, slower lowering.
