# Dumbbell Bench Press

**Targets:** chest, triceps, front shoulders · **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells, flat bench · **Used in:** Day A · **Reps:** 3 × 8–12

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Chest Press](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/19/chest-press/) · [ACE — Incline Chest Press](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/25/incline-chest-press/) (swap)

## Setup
- Sit on the end of the bench with the dumbbells standing on your thighs.
- Lie back and use your knees to "kick" the dumbbells up to your chest.
- Feet flat on the floor, shoulder blades pulled **back and down** into the bench, slight natural arch.

## How to do it
1. Press the dumbbells up over your chest until your arms are straight (don't clank them together).
2. Lower slowly (2–3 seconds) until the dumbbells are beside your chest, elbows about **45°** from your body.
3. Press back up.
4. Finish: bring the dumbbells to your chest, then sit up using your knees, or lower them to the floor carefully.

## Cues
- Shoulder blades stay pinned the whole time.
- Wrists stacked over elbows.
- Push yourself away from the dumbbells.

## Common mistakes
- Elbows flared to 90° → stresses the shoulders.
- Bouncing at the bottom → control the lowering.
- Shoulders rolling forward at the top → keep your blades pinned.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** lighter dumbbells, or the [push-up](push-up.md).
- **Harder:** heavier weight, pause at the bottom, or incline bench (30–45°) for more upper chest.
