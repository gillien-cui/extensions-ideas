# Goblet Squat

**Targets:** quads, glutes, core · **Equipment:** 1 dumbbell · **Used in:** Day A · **Reps:** 3 × 8–12

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Goblet Squat](https://acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/362/goblet-squat) · [NASM — Goblet Squat](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/goblet-squat)

## Setup
- Hold one dumbbell vertically against your chest, both hands cupping the top end, like holding a goblet.
- Feet shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly (15–30°).

## How to do it
1. Brace your core (as if about to be poked in the stomach).
2. Sit **down and back** between your heels, knees tracking over your toes.
3. Go as low as you can while keeping your back straight. Aim for hips at or below knee height.
4. Push through your whole foot to stand up. Squeeze your glutes at the top.

## Cues
- "Elbows inside the knees" at the bottom.
- Chest up, dumbbell stays touching your chest.
- Exhale on the way up.

## Common mistakes
- Heels lifting → shift weight back, or put small plates under your heels.
- Knees caving inward → push your knees out over your toes.
- Rounding your lower back at the bottom → stop slightly higher.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** bodyweight squat to a bench (box squat).
- **Harder:** heavier dumbbell, 3-second lowering, or move to a barbell back squat.
