# Glute Bridge

**Targets:** glutes, hamstrings · **Equipment:** mat; barbell or dumbbell optional · **Used in:** swap for hip thrust · **Reps:** 3 × 8–12 (weighted) or 2 × 15 (bodyweight)

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Glute Bridge](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/49/glute-bridge/)

## Setup
- Lie on your back on the mat, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart, heels about a hand's length from your glutes.
- Weighted: rest a dumbbell or padded barbell across your hip crease and hold it in place.

## How to do it
1. Brace your core and press your lower back gently into the floor.
2. Drive through your heels and lift your hips until there's a straight line from shoulders to knees.
3. Squeeze your glutes for 1–2 seconds at the top.
4. Lower slowly.

## Cues
- Push your heels through the floor.
- Ribs down; the squeeze comes from your glutes, not your back.

## Common mistakes
- Overarching at the top.
- Feeling it mostly in the hamstrings → bring your feet slightly closer.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** bodyweight only.
- **Harder:** heavier weight, single-leg, or progress to the [hip thrust](hip-thrust.md) (bench gives more range of motion).
