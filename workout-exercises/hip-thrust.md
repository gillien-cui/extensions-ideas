# Hip Thrust (臀推)

**Targets:** glutes, hamstrings · **Equipment:** barbell + pad, bench · **Used in:** Day B · **Reps:** 3 × 8–12

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Understanding the Barbell Hip Thrust](https://www.acefitness.org/continuing-education/certified/november-2019/7403/understanding-the-barbell-hip-thrust/) · [ACE — Elevated Glute Bridge](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/367/elevated-glute-bridge/)

## Setup
- Sit on the floor with your upper back (just below the shoulder blades) against the long side of a bench.
- Roll a padded barbell over your hips so it sits in the hip crease.
- Feet flat, about hip-width apart, close enough that your shins are vertical at the top.

## How to do it
1. Tuck your chin slightly and brace.
2. Drive through your heels and lift your hips until your body is a straight line from shoulders to knees.
3. Squeeze your glutes hard at the top for 1 second. Ribs down, no arching.
4. Lower under control until your hips are just above the floor.

## Cues
- "Look forward, not at the ceiling": your eyes follow your torso.
- Slight posterior pelvic tilt at the top (tuck your tailbone).
- Push your knees out slightly.

## Common mistakes
- Arching your lower back to get higher → stop at a straight line and squeeze your glutes.
- Feet too far away (hamstring cramp) or too close (quads take over) → adjust until your shins are vertical at the top.
- Bar rolling → use a pad and hold the bar with both hands.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** [glute bridge](glute-bridge.md) on the floor, or a dumbbell on your hips.
- **Harder:** more weight, 2-second pause at the top, single-leg hip thrust.
