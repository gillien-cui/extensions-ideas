# Lat Pulldown

**Targets:** lats, upper back, biceps · **Equipment:** cable machine (high pulley) + bar · **Used in:** swap for row · **Reps:** 2 × 12–15

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Seated Lat Pulldown](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/158/seated-lat-pulldown/) · [ACE — Kneeling Lat Pulldown](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/35/kneeling-lat-pulldown/) (if there's no seat)

**Climber note:** this is very similar to climbing pulls. Use it only occasionally and keep it light. The [seated cable row](seated-cable-row.md) or [face pull](face-pull.md) is usually the better choice for you.

## Setup
- Grab the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width, palms facing away.
- Sit (or kneel) with thighs secured under the pad, or kneel facing the machine if there's no seat.
- Arms straight, slight lean back (no more than about 30°).

## How to do it
1. Pull your shoulder blades down first.
2. Pull the bar to your upper chest, driving your elbows down toward your back pockets.
3. Squeeze for 1 second.
4. Let the bar rise slowly until your arms are straight.

## Cues
- Chest up to meet the bar.
- Elbows down and back, not behind your head.

## Common mistakes
- Leaning way back and turning it into a row.
- Pulling behind your neck (hard on the shoulders).
- Using momentum.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** lighter weight.
- **Harder:** slower lowering (3 seconds).
