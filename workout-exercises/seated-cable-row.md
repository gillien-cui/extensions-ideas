# Seated Cable Row

**Targets:** mid back, lats, rear shoulders, biceps · **Equipment:** cable machine (low pulley) + V-handle · **Used in:** Day C (light) · **Reps:** 2 × 12–15

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Seated Row](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/48/seated-row/)

**Climber note:** you already pull a lot, so keep this **light**, about 70% effort. It's here for back posture and balance, not for building more pulling strength. Skip it or replace it with a [face pull](face-pull.md) if you climbed hard in the last 24–48 hours.

## Setup
- Attach a V-handle to the low pulley. Sit facing the machine, feet on the platform, knees slightly bent.
- Grab the handle, sit tall with a straight back, arms extended.

## How to do it
1. Pull your shoulder blades back, then pull the handle to your belly button.
2. Elbows travel close to your body.
3. Squeeze your shoulder blades together for 1 second.
4. Let your arms extend slowly, letting your shoulders reach forward a little at the end.

## Cues
- Chest up, torso stays still (only a small lean).
- Lead with your elbows, not your hands.
- Shoulders down, away from your ears.

## Common mistakes
- Rocking back and forth to move the weight.
- Rounding your back on the return.
- Shrugging.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** lighter weight.
- **Harder:** pause 2 seconds at the squeeze, single-arm row.
