# Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift (Single-leg RDL)

**Targets:** hamstrings, glutes, balance · **Equipment:** 1–2 dumbbells · **Used in:** swap for reverse lunge · **Reps:** 2 × 12–15 per leg

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Single-leg Romanian Deadlift](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/329/single-leg-romanian-deadlift/) · [ACE — Single-arm Single-leg RDL (dumbbell)](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/127/single-arm-single-leg-romanian-dead-lift/)

## Setup
- Stand on one leg with a soft bend in that knee. Hold a dumbbell in the **opposite** hand (or one in each hand).

## How to do it
1. Hinge at the hip: push your hips back while your free leg extends straight behind you.
2. Lower the dumbbell along your standing leg until your torso is close to parallel with the floor, or until you feel a strong hamstring stretch.
3. Keep your hips square to the floor, not opening toward the ceiling.
4. Drive your hips forward to stand back up, squeezing the glute of the standing leg.

## Cues
- Body moves like a seesaw: head and back heel move together in one straight line.
- Back flat, neck neutral.
- Point the toes of your back foot at the floor (this keeps your hips square).

## Common mistakes
- Rounding your back to reach lower → only go as low as your hamstrings allow.
- Bending your standing knee a lot → that turns it into a squat.
- Losing balance → slow down, or lightly touch a rack with your free hand.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** kickstand RDL (back toes stay lightly on the floor), or hold a rack for balance.
- **Harder:** heavier dumbbell, pause at the bottom.
