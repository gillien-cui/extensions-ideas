# Dumbbell Shoulder Press

**Targets:** shoulders, triceps, upper back · **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells, bench with upright back · **Used in:** Day C · **Reps:** 3 × 8–12

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Seated Shoulder Press](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/186/seated-shoulder-press/)

## Setup
- Set the bench upright (about 80–90°). Sit with your back against it and feet flat.
- Bring the dumbbells to shoulder height, palms facing forward or slightly toward each other, elbows slightly in front of your body.

## How to do it
1. Brace your core; ribs down.
2. Press the dumbbells up until your arms are straight overhead, dumbbells slightly toward each other.
3. Lower under control back to shoulder height.

## Cues
- Don't arch your lower back to push the weight up.
- Elbows slightly forward (not flared straight to the sides) is easier on the shoulders.
- Head stays neutral.

## Common mistakes
- Big back arch → lighter weight, brace harder.
- Partial reps → lower all the way to shoulder level.
- Elbows flaring straight out to the sides.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** lighter dumbbells, or alternate arms.
- **Harder:** standing press (more core), or single-arm press.

## Climber note
Overhead pressing balances all the pulling from climbing. Start light: shoulders tired from climbing tend to be weaker here than you expect.
