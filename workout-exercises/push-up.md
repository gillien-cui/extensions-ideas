# Push-up

**Targets:** chest, triceps, front shoulders, core · **Equipment:** bodyweight · **Used in:** Day B (and Session 1) · **Reps:** 3 × 8–12

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Push-Up](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/41/push-up/) · [NASM — Push-Up](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/push-up)

## Setup
- Hands slightly wider than shoulder-width, under or slightly outside your shoulders.
- Legs straight, feet together or hip-width apart. Body forms a straight plank from head to heels.

## How to do it
1. Brace your core and squeeze your glutes.
2. Lower your chest toward the floor, elbows angled about **45°** from your body (an arrow shape, not a T).
3. Go down until your chest is a fist's height from the floor.
4. Press the floor away to return to the top, fully straightening your arms.

## Cues
- "Screw your hands into the floor" (turns on your lats, keeps elbows in).
- Body moves as one piece, no sagging hips.
- Exhale on the way up.

## Common mistakes
- Hips sagging or piking → squeeze your glutes, brace.
- Elbows flared to 90° → tuck to about 45°; this is easier on the shoulders.
- Half reps → use an easier version to get full range.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** hands on a bench or barbell in a rack (higher = easier), or [ACE — Bent-knee Push-Up](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/13/bent-knee-push-up/).
- **Harder:** feet raised on a bench, a 3-second lowering, a weight plate on your back.
- **Progression:** once 3 × 12 is easy, use feet raised or move to the [dumbbell bench press](dumbbell-bench-press.md) for loading.
