# Crunch

**Targets:** abs (rectus abdominis) · **Equipment:** mat · **Used in:** Day B · **Reps:** 2 × 12–15

**Demo (photos + video):** [ACE — Crunch](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/52/crunch/) · [ACE — Reverse Crunch](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/76/reverse-crunch/) (variation)

## Setup
- Lie on your back on the mat, knees bent, feet flat, heels about 30–45 cm from your tailbone.
- Hands lightly behind your head (supporting it, not pulling) or crossed on your chest.

## How to do it
1. Exhale and curl your ribs toward your hips until your shoulder blades lift off the mat.
2. Pause 1 second at the top.
3. Lower **slowly** back down.

## Cues
- Think "curl", not "sit up". It's a short movement.
- Keep a fist-sized gap between your chin and chest.
- Slow and controlled. Rushing lets the hip flexors take over and strains your lower back.

## Common mistakes
- Yanking on your neck with your hands.
- Going too fast.
- Lifting all the way up into a sit-up.

## Easier / harder
- **Easier:** smaller range, arms by your sides.
- **Harder:** hold a weight plate on your chest, or do a cable crunch (kneeling, rope from high pulley).
