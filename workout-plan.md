# Workout Plan — Ground Rules

Simple, low-stress strength training: **3 days a week, 3 exercises a day, about 30 minutes.**
The goal is the benefits of strength training without overloading the body.

- Exercise library (how-to + demo links): [`workout-exercises/`](workout-exercises/README.md)
- Log: [Training log](#training-log) at the bottom of this file

---

## 1. Ground rules

1. **3 sessions a week**, with at least one rest day between sessions (e.g. Mon / Wed / Fri).
2. **3 exercises a session:** one leg, one push, one pull or core.
3. **30 minutes total:** a 5-minute warm-up and about 25 minutes of work.
4. **Effort:** finish each set with **1–2 reps left in the tank** (about 90% effort, RPE 8–9). Don't go to failure.
5. **Form first.** If form breaks down, the set is over.
6. **Log every set:** weight, reps, and how it felt (see the [feel scale](#feel-scale)).
7. **Pain ≠ effort.** Stop if anything hurts sharply or in a joint. Muscle burn is fine.

## 2. Climbing adjustments

Climbing already covers a lot of pulling, so the gym work leans **push-heavy**:

- 3 push sessions a week (chest + shoulders), only **1 light pull** (row), plus **face pulls** for shoulder health.
- Face pulls and push work balance the shoulders after climbing. Climbers often end up with forward-rounded shoulders.
- Don't do the row day the day before a hard climbing session.
- If your forearms or fingers are tired from climbing, that's fine: these sessions barely use grip.

## 3. Rep schemes

| Scheme | Use for | Rest between sets |
|---|---|---|
| **3 × 8–12** | Main lifts (squat, hip thrust, presses) | 60–90 s |
| **2 × 12–15** | Lighter / smaller moves (face pull, row, crunch, lunges) | 45–60 s |

## 4. Progression rule (double progression)

1. Pick a weight you can lift for the **bottom** of the rep range (e.g. 8 reps) with 1–2 reps left.
2. Each session, try to add a rep or two.
3. When you hit the **top** of the range (12, or 15) on **every set** with good form, increase the weight by the **smallest step available** (about 5%, e.g. +1–2.5 kg dumbbells, +2.5–5 kg barbell).
4. Start back at the bottom of the range with the new weight. Repeat.

**First 2 weeks:** use lighter weights (RPE 6–7) while you find your starting numbers and get used to the routine.

**Every 6–8 weeks:** take an easy week (about 60% weight, or 1 set per exercise).

## 5. Warm-up (5 minutes)

1. 2 minutes of easy cardio (bike, brisk walk, or jumping jacks)
2. 10 bodyweight squats
3. 10 arm circles each way + 10 band pull-aparts or wall slides
4. **1 light warm-up set** (about 50% weight, 8 reps) of the first exercise

## 6. The 3-day rotation

Rotate A → B → C. If you miss a day, pick up where you left off. Don't double up.

## 7. Today's workout — Session 1 (easy start)

**Day A, beginner version.** About 20 minutes. Effort **6–7 out of 10**: you should feel like you could do 3–4 more reps. Today's job is to learn the moves and find your starting weights, not to push hard.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Weight | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Warm-up (above) | — | — | — |
| 1 | [Goblet squat](workout-exercises/goblet-squat.md) | 2 × 10 | Light dumbbell (try 8–12 kg) | 60 s |
| 2 | [Push-up](workout-exercises/push-up.md) | 2 × 10 | Bodyweight (hands on a bench if 10 is hard) | 60 s |
| 3 | [Face pull](workout-exercises/face-pull.md) | 2 × 15 | Light cable | 45 s |

After each set, write down the weight and how many more reps you think you had left. That's how you'll set next session's weights.

## Training log

**One row per set**, so you can see how each weight felt at each rep count.

### Feel scale

Rate each set right after you finish it. Your rating is the number of reps you could still have done (RIR = reps in reserve).

| Feel | Reps left | Meaning | What to do next time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 😌 **Easy** | 4+ | Could keep going for a while | Add weight |
| 🙂 **Moderate** | 3 | Working, but comfortable | Add reps (or weight if at the top of the range) |
| 😤 **Hard** | 1–2 | Target zone (about 90% effort) | Add 1–2 reps, same weight |
| 🥵 **Max** | 0 | Couldn't do another rep with good form | Same weight and reps until it feels Hard |

Use `?` if you didn't rate a set.
